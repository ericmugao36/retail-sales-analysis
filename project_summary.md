# Retail Sales Analysis & Business Reporting

**Turning sales records into better business decisions.**

## The Challenge

Many small businesses collect sales information but struggle to turn it into useful insights. Transaction records may contain duplicates, inconsistent entries and missing information, making it difficult to understand actual business performance.

## The Solution

I developed an end-to-end retail sales analysis workflow using Python, Excel and Power BI.

The process transforms raw sales records into organised data, financial summaries, visual reports and practical business recommendations.

## What the Analysis Reveals

Using a simulated Kenyan retail dataset, the analysis identified:

- **KSh 2.51 million** in total revenue.
- **KSh 730,249.50** in total profit.
- **29.15%** overall profit margin.
- Meru as the highest-revenue branch.
- Household products as the leading revenue category.
- February as the strongest sales month.

## Business Value

The analysis demonstrates how businesses can:

- Compare performance across branches.
- Identify profitable and underperforming product categories.
- Monitor revenue and profit trends.
- Improve stock planning.
- Investigate pricing and cost issues.
- Make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions.
